About Me

Hey, I'm Shubham — a pre-final year CSE student at NIT Patna, carrying a 9.27 CGPA and a habit of building things I wish already existed. EduStack started because I was tired of bouncing between ten different tabs every time I needed notes, a coding platform, or a previous year's paper.

My stack is Node.js, Express, MongoDB, and MySQL on the backend, with C++ for DSA. Most of what's on this site is stuff I'm using myself right now for my own placement prep. If it helps you the way it's helped me, that's the whole point.

As a Computer Science and Engineering student, I'm always eager to explore new technology to get exposure and experience from them to solve real-world problems. I am excited to apply my knowledge and skills to contribute to innovative projects and collaborate with like-minded individuals in the software industry. On a personal level, I'm passionate about self-improvement and personal growth.
